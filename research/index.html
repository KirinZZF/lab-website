Our work follows the life of a fault: noticing that a turbine has stopped behaving normally, finding which component is responsible, estimating how long it has left, and choosing how the turbine should be operated in the meantime. Quantum machine learning and digital twins run across all of it.

Methods are developed and validated on real SCADA data from operational wind farms, with leakage-free evaluation across turbines and farms.
