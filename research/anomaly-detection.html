A normal behaviour model learns how a healthy turbine relates wind, power, temperatures and commands. When the turbine stops behaving that way, the gap between what the model expects and what the turbine reports grows, and an adaptive threshold decides when that gap is a fault rather than noise. No fault labels are needed for training.

We use quantum machine learning for the normal behaviour model. Variational quantum circuits embedded in the LSTM gates (QLSTM) capture high-order interactions among SCADA variables in Hilbert space with few parameters, and the number of input variables sets the number of qubits.
